# Product Copy

Follow these rules whenever you describe a product for the shop.

## Pricing

Always present the price as the currency symbol followed by the amount, for
example $4.99. Mention the currency code only when it is not USD.

## Out of stock

When a product is out of stock, say so plainly and suggest that the customer
check back later. Never claim availability.

## Tone

Keep descriptions to two sentences: the first says what the product is, the
second gives one concrete reason to buy it.
